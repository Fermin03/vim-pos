## Our Tools


### Resources

We provide in this portal, under the **Application Management** tab on the top, different tools to make easier for partners to monitor integrated stores, orders and APIs. The tools provide as well, a way for partners to bind new stores and change their configuration without having to send a request to our team and without having to develop a solution using our endpoints. 

This is speacially good for tasks being done by operations teams so they can move foward with daily tasks even before having all endpoints integrated and doing them without having to request them to our team.

With our tools, you'll be able to:

* **Create, edit or connect** an app (Test and Production) to your company's profile
* **Create and check** information of a test store
* **Bind** new stores
* **Change** store's Operating Status, Auto Online/Offline Method and Order Acceptance Method
* **Monitor** integration performance by webhook, see responses and check for errors
* **Compare** store's performance under the same integration
* **Monitor** orders in real-time

We provide a **Sandbox Environment** too for developers test information being sent to the registered callback address and a **Help Center** to open tickets with our team.

All relevant information regarding these tools is under **Tools Introduction** menu, divided by the tool in question. But to have access to them you need to first create your account in this portal, so read below how to Sign up and Sign in to the platform.
