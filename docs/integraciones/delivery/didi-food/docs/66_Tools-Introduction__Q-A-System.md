## Q&A System

### FAQ

We are always updating our **FAQ**, so if you have any questions during the process, we recommend that you check the FAQ first. It's divided by big topics and divided by subtopics to make easier for you to find the answer there.

### Help Center

If you don't find the answer for your question in the FAQ, you can use our **Help Center**. We welcome questions from our partners and in order to improve the efficiency and accuracy of answering questions, we built this system. Since different question need to be solved by different content, sometimes by different teams, the **Help Center** is the most efficient way to get the answers you are looking for. We'll respond within 3 business days and if your problem is not resolved, you can continue to ask questions.

When using the **Help Center** we ask you to to add questions in English and to get your problem solved faster, add as much details as possible. When opportune, provide us with the IDs for the app, store, order or menu, telling when the problem occured, if you already did any debugging and how it went and if you were able to similate the error again. And be ready to provide us with images or videos showing us the problem. This could be very helpful on solving it.

Please follow up on the replies in a timely manner as we may have additional questions to be able to solve your question.
