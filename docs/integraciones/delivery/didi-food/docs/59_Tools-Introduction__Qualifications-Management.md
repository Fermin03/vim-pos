## Qualifications Management

Now that you have access to the Application Management section of the platform, we need you to apply for the  [Qualifications Management](https://developer.didi-food.com/en-US/app/qualifications/statistical). With this, you'll create an account for you company under where you'll be able to create test and production apps to develop an integration.

The first step is to **fill the form** with the information about your company. Is adivasable to use the same information used when signing our NDA because after receiving it, our team will audit the information to accept or refuse the application. To be accepted, have completed the previous steps to this one described in **Integration Guide > Basic Process Overview**.

You'll receive a notification about the result of this audit so you can try again or move to the next step.

> **NOTE:**
> If you want to understand better why your qualification failed, you can contect our team.

If you don't complete the qualification, you'll have limited acces to some of our tools.

After the qualification process is done, you can:
* Create a new test or production app or connect an already created app (When the app was created before August 2021 by DiDi's team). Read how to do it in **Tools Introduction > Application Management**.
* Add users administrate all apps of your company or just one of them. See how this works in **Tools Introduction > Permissions Management**.
