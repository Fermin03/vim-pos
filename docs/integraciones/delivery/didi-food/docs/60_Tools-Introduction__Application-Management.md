## Application Management

Now that you have your qualification approved, you can **create and administrate** your applications, the services developed with APIs in DiDi's system.

### Creating an App

Enter Application Management tool and follow the steps below:

![Limitations: B-App Versions Vs. Integration](http://img0.didiglobal.com/static/soda_public/do1_hPHt2g2KzRqvrpWGesFb)

We ask you to follow a format for the **Application Name** that helps our operations team understand all the apps created and provide you with faster help: CountryCode_AppType_AppName (Usually the name of the POS or Restaurant). Ex: The testing app for McDonalds in Mexico = MX_T_McDonalds.

| **Country** | **CountryCode** | 
|-------------|-----------------|
| Brazil      | BR              |
| Chile       | CL              |
| Colombia    | CO              |
| Costa Rica  | CR              |
| Mexico      | MX              |
| Japan       | JP              |

| **AppType** | **Environment**        | 
|-------------|------------------------|
| T           | Test environment       |
| P           | Production environment |

> **NOTE**:
> After creating the app, you may want to give access to it to other collaborators. Check **Tools Introduction > Permiossions Management** for that.


## Connecting an App

If your app was created before August 2021, you may not have access to it in this management area. To do so, first you need to completed the steps in **Qualifications Management** (contact our team if you're not sure if your company already has a registration). After getting you application audit, you'll be able to connect your app following the steps below and you'll be able to manage your app using our portal.

![Limitations: B-App Versions Vs. Integration](http://img0.didiglobal.com/static/soda_public/do1_P5LtQSNyMQ9y53VenHW7)

> **NOTE**:
> After connection the app, you may want to give access to it to other collaborators. Check **Tools Introduction > Permiossions Management** for that.


## Editing an App

After creating or connection an app, you can edit some of its details. To do so, **click on View Details** of the app you want to add in the apps list. Now, **click on Edit** to change the name, profile or callback address or add new email addresses. After the changes, **click on Confirm** to save the changes.
